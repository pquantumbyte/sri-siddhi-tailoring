Our Story

A family craft, stitched with patience.

Sri Siddhi Ladies Tailoring began as a single sewing machine in a family home — and grew, one perfectly fitted blouse at a time, into a trusted studio for brides, working women and families across the region.

We measure twice, cut once, and finish every hem by hand. No two bodies are the same, so no two garments leave our studio identical.

  • Hand-finished seams & hems
  • Bridal trial fittings included
  • Fabric sourcing guidance
What We Stitch

Tailoring for every occasion

From daily-wear blouses to elaborate bridal ensembles — each order is cut to your exact measurements and finished by hand.

Blouse Stitching

Custom blouses in boat neck, halter, backless & princess-cut styles, fitted to perfection.

Saree Fall & Pico

Same-day fall stitching and pico edging so your saree drapes and sits exactly right.

Bridal & Wedding Wear

Full bridal ensembles with hand embroidery, dupatta setting, and multiple trial fittings.

Alterations & Refitting

Resizing, waist adjustment and repair work on ready-mades and heirloom garments alike.

Kids & Pattu Wear

Pattu langa-voni, frocks and festive wear for little ones, sized for growing room.

Aari, Hand & Computer Embroidery
